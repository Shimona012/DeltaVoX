    Speech Delivery Rubric & Coaching Guide

    The four core vocal dimensions we evaluate to help you become a compelling, expressive speaker.

    1. Pitch Expressiveness & Melody (35%)

    What we listen for: How naturally your voice rises and falls across phrases to emphasize key thoughts and keep listeners captivated.

    Common stumbling block: Speaking on a flat, single tone (monotone) without natural vocal melody.

    2. Vocal Projection & Energy (25%)

    What we listen for: Steady, confident voice volume throughout whole sentences, ensuring your last words carry just as clearly as your first.

    Common stumbling block: Trailing off or losing breath and energy toward the end of important statements.

    3. Speaking Rhythm & Tempo (25%)

    What we listen for: A consistent, comfortable pace that allows the audience to follow your thoughts easily without feeling rushed.

    Common stumbling block: Articulating words too quickly or stumbling when transitioning between ideas.

    4. Purposeful Pauses & Breathing (15%)

    What we listen for: Intentional silences that let big points land with impact and give your delivery breathing room.

    Common stumbling block: Stalling mid-sentence or trailing into prolonged, hesitant dead silence.

    🎙️ How to Use SpeechContrastive Engine

    1. Step 1 (Dock A — Target Benchmark): Select a reference speech (e.g. Civic Oratory Benchmark) or upload your own reference file. Click the [+] Target Benchmark Profile to view vocal stats (pitch, cadence, pace).
    2. Step 2 (Dock B — Your Voice Take): Practice using one of the pre-recorded benchmark take levels (L0 to L4), record your voice live using your microphone, or upload any audio file.
    3. Step 3 (Prepared Script & Topic): Optionally type what you planned to say, click Civic Speech, or enter any topic (e.g. "Dinosaurs") and click ✨ Generate Practice Script. The AI will compare what you actually said against your script to find stumbles and fumbles!
    4. Step 4 (Language & Accent): Choose your Speaking Style, Speaker Accent (General American, British, Indian English, etc.), and Feedback Language (English, Hindi, Gujarati, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, etc.).
    5. Step 5 (Evaluate): Click Evaluate My Speech Delivery. View your overall score, melody pitch curve, top coaching tips, detected stumbles, and word adherence percentage!

    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Q: Where do I change the feedback language and speaking style?

    In the Speech Studio right above the green "Evaluate" button, use the Speaking Style, Speaker Accent, and Feedback Language dropdowns.

    Q: What are the levels L0 through L4 in the Speech Library?

    Level 0 is the gold-standard benchmark. Level 1 has subtle pacing drift, Level 2 minor compression, Level 3 moderate energy fade, and Level 4 severe flat monotone or hesitation stalls.

    Q: Is my voice recording saved on any server?

    No. Zero audio is stored. Speech recordings are held only in volatile memory for feature extraction and immediately destroyed.

    Our Mission & Approach

    SpeechContrastive Engine bridges the gap between raw transcription and rhetorical delivery. Rather than merely grading your grammar, our engine aligns your vocal pitch contour, pause rhythm, and energy envelope directly against gold-standard rhetorical and civic oratory benchmarks.

    Core Acoustic Architecture

    • Dynamic Time Warping (DTW): Time-aligns non-linear vocal speeds across spoken sentences.
    • Syllable Nuclei Detection: Calculates cadence, pause structure, and rushing rhythm.
    • Multilingual Gemini Multimodal: Verbatim phoneme transcription and multi-accent rhetorical coaching.
    • Zero-Persistence Privacy: Vocal takes exist solely in temporary volatile RAM.
